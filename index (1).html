About the platform

One workspace for every role in patient care

Smart Nurse Management System brings hospital administrators, doctors and nurses onto a single, connected platform. Instead of scattered paperwork, phone calls and disconnected spreadsheets, every role works from the same real-time view of patients, tasks and instructions.

The goal is simple: reduce the daily workflow complexity that nurses face, and make coordination between doctors, nurses and administrators faster, clearer and easier to track — from patient intake to shift handover.

Purpose-built tools that mirror how hospital teams actually work together.

Daily tasks are organized and prioritized automatically, so nurses always know what needs attention next.

How it works

From hospital registration to completed care

A single, traceable path — every step below feeds directly into the next.

  1. 01

    Hospital Registration

    A hospital submits its registration request to join the platform.

  2. 02

    Super Admin Verification

    Anthropic's Smart Nurse team verifies hospital details before approval.

  3. 03

    Hospital Admin Account

    An approved hospital receives its admin account to manage the workspace.

  4. 04

    Doctor / Nurse Accounts

    The hospital admin invites and creates accounts for doctors and nurses.

  5. 05

    Doctor Adds Patient Information

    Doctors record patient details and enter care instructions.

  6. 06

    Admin Assigns Nurse + Schedule

    The admin assigns a nurse and shift schedule to each patient.

  7. 07

    Nurse Receives Tasks

    The assigned nurse sees a prioritized task list for their shift.

  8. 08

    Nurse Completes Tasks

    Tasks are updated and observations are logged as care is delivered.

  9. 09

    Doctor / Admin Receives Updates

    Progress and observations flow back to the doctor and admin in real time.

Security

Access designed around roles, not guesswork

Every account only reaches the hospital data and records it's authorized to see.

Role-Based Access Control

Admins, doctors and nurses each see only the tools and records their role allows.

Hospital-Level Data Isolation

Each hospital's staff, patients and records are kept separate from every other hospital.

Secure Authentication

Accounts are protected with secure sign-in built for healthcare environments.

Protected Patient Information

Patient records are only reachable by the doctors and nurses assigned to that patient.

Secure Staff Invitations

Doctors and nurses join a hospital only through invitations issued by its admin.

Audit Logs

Key actions across the workspace are recorded, supporting accountability and review.

Controlled Document Access

Medical documents are shared only with the authorized staff involved in a patient's care.

Continuous Improvement

Access rules and safeguards are reviewed and strengthened as the platform evolves.

Not a substitute for clinical judgment

Smart Nurse Management System does not independently diagnose patients, prescribe medicines or make clinical decisions. It is a coordination and workflow tool — all medical decisions remain with authorized doctors and healthcare professionals.
